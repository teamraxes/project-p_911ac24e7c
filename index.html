№ 04 · Three generations · One block

“Same family, same block of 40th Street, same line of work — since 1980.”

Moran Supply opened on 40th Street in 1980 as a single counter selling brass fittings to East Bay plumbers. Forty-six years and three generations later, the counter is still here. So is the family.

We have outlasted three big-box stores within a mile of our door because what we sell isn’t a price match — it’s a person who has been quoting Kohler since the second Reagan administration and can tell you, on the phone, whether a part is on the shelf or three days out.

We don’t plumb your house. We supply the people who do, and we sell directly to the people whose houses they’re plumbing. That’s the whole business. It has been for forty-six years.
